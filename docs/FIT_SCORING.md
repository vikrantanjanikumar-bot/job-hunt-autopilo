# Match score (stage 4)

Score each posting out of 100 after reading the full description, never from the title.

| Part | Points | What earns them |
|---|---|---|
| Role match | 30 | the duties are what the candidate does now or did recently |
| Seniority | 20 | years asked and level sit within about one step of the candidate |
| Evidence | 20 | the top three requirements each have a proof point in the résumé |
| Location and priority | 15 | preferred region or employer; relocation realistic |
| Domain | 10 | same or adjacent industry |
| Freshness | 5 | posted within 14 days, still open on the employer's site |

## Blockers (cap the score at 45 and say why)

- A certificate, tool, degree or number of domain years marked mandatory that the candidate
  does not hold.
- Existing right to work, residency, citizenship or security clearance the candidate lacks.
- A required language the candidate does not speak.
- Pay stated below the candidate's floor.

"Preferred", "a plus" and "or equivalent" are gaps, not blockers: note them and carry on.

## Verify before scoring

Open the employer's own page where possible. Skip a posting that is closed, is already in
the tracker under another link, comes from an "employer" that cannot be identified, or asks
for money at any point.

## Output per posting

Fit, the blockers in the posting's own words, the résumé variant to use, and one line on why.
Above 50 with no blocker: apply. Otherwise record the reason and move on.
